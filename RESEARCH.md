# Eden Margao: public website review

Reviewed 8 October 2026. This project is an independent portfolio concept by Pritam Badagi. It is not commissioned by, affiliated with, or connected to Eden Salon's booking system.

## What was verified

- Matching business listing: https://salonist.io/cs/s/EDENSALONHOMESTAYPVTLTD
- Existing booking page: https://salonist.io/cs/booking/EDENSALONHOMESTAYPVTLTD
- The location is Matruchaya Building, opposite New Era High School, Malbhat, Margao, Goa 403601.
- The live customer flow offers individual/group appointments, service selection, professional selection, a date calendar, available times, and a price summary.
- No real appointment was submitted and no account was accessed during research.
- A separate official Margao website was not verified. Similarly named Eden salon websites belong to other locations.

## Observable opportunities

- Clearer durations: some massage descriptions specify one or two hours while their displayed service duration is 15 minutes.
- Consistent published opening hours and bookable times.
- Stylist profiles that communicate specialities, rather than names and generic avatars alone.
- Clear cancellation and appointment-management guidance before booking.

The existing platform may already support cancellation and an administrator calendar behind sign-in. Those features were not visible to this review, so this project does not claim that the salon lacks them.

## How the concept differs

The concept includes a sample service menu with realistic durations, fictional stylist profiles, date and time availability, a booking confirmation, private guest booking management, cancellation, and a protected administrator calendar. Service names, prices, staff identities, operating hours, and cancellation rules in the demo are examples, not verified salon policies. Images illustrate a concept and are not presented as photos of the actual Margao premises.

The local Firestore emulator stores demo records. No demo booking is sent to the real salon.
